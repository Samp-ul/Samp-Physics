### Your builds can <u>*finally*</u> move, time to make some pods.

SampPhysics is the physics engine behind Samp Coasters. It turns blocks into pods that fall, tip over, float and bump into things! It runs on Fabric for Minecraft 26.1.2, 26.2, and 26.3.

## Installing

**Playing Samp Coasters 1.2.0?** You already have it, it comes inside the jar!

1. Grab the jar for your Minecraft version from [CurseForge](LINK-CURSEFORGE).
2. Put it in your `mods` folder along with [Fabric API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fabric-api).
3. Playing on a server? It needs to be on the server and on every player's client.

## Making a mod with it

SampPhysics is a library, so your mod can make its own pods!
